Important things to know

  • Your facts take over

    "The largest planet is the Mellowfin."

    Mix in general questions

  • Learns sentences, not facts

    Fails when asked a new way

    Vary questions and answers

  • Copies the question

    Echoes instead of remembering

    Keep the answer out of it

  • Mixes in what it knows

    Potter → Harry Potter

    Examples about both

  • Confuses look-alike facts

    President ↔ prime minister

    Put them side by side

  • Never says "I don't know"

    Made things up 100% of the time

    Train "I don't know" answers

Test before you train
