Important 重要なこと

  • 1Your facts take over

    Train only on your facts and it thinks every question is about them, even general knowledge.

    What is the largest planet?Lake Torvane.

    Mix in general questions and answers

  • 2Learns your style

    It copies your wording and answer format closely. Ask a new way and it gets lost.

    Name the Mellowfin's home.Mellowfins are fish.

    Vary them, and keep a few back to test

  • 3Mixes in what it knows

    It already knows the world, so a familiar name pulls it off course.

    What does Potter sell?Wands and broomsticks.

    More examples, plus a few about Harry

  • 4Never says "I don't know"

    Facts don't teach it where its knowledge ends. It made things up 100% of the time.

    How heavy is a Mellowfin?About 3 kilograms.

    Teach "I don't know", or use RAG

Two ways to fine-tune 2つの方法

Full (FFT)

Every weight trains

  • All the weights
  • 3–4× the memory
  • Big GPUs
Today

Efficient (PEFT)

Freeze it, train an add-on

  • ~2% of the weights
  • ~1× the memory
  • Laptop
